# FPL reporting verification rules

Before each report, check official event flags (`is_current`, `finished`, `data_checked`) and match results. Do not call a gameweek finished when fixtures remain.

Before repeating an injury concern, verify dated team news and official match appearances/minutes. A player who played after an injury report must not be described as still injured solely from that older report.

Distinguish verified public post-deadline picks from authenticated pre-deadline squad, bank, free transfers and selling prices. Never assume a proposed transfer was executed.

Use source retrieval timestamps and flag stale or unavailable fields. Avoid duplicate unchanged alerts.
